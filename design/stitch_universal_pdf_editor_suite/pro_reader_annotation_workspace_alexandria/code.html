Series A Preferred Stock Term Sheet

4.1 Pre-Money Valuation & Financing Structure

The financing shall consist of the issuance and sale of Series A Preferred Stock at a pre-money enterprise valuation of $42,000,000 (Forty-Two Million USD) , exclusive of the unallocated employee option expansion pool defined under Appendix B. Aggregate gross proceeds from the sale shall equal $12,500,000.

4.2 Liquidation Preference & Waterfall Distribution

In the event of any liquidation, dissolution, or winding up of the Corporation, the proceeds shall be distributed first to the holders of the Series A Preferred Stock. Each Series A holder shall be entitled to receive an amount per share equal to two times (2.0x) original purchase price one times (1.0x) plus accrued dividends, prior and in preference to any declaration or payment of dividends to common stock. After such initial preferential payout, the remaining assets shall be allocated pro-rata.

4.3 Governance & Board Representation

The Board of Directors shall be reconstituted immediately upon closing to consist of five (5) members designated as follows:

  • • Two (2) directors designated by the Common Stockholders, initially designated by the Founder Group.
  • • Two (2) directors designated by the Series A Preferred Stockholders, led by Apex Horizon Capital.
  • • One (1) independent director with industry experience, jointly appointed by mutual consent of both parties.
